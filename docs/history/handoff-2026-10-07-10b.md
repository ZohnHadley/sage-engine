## 2. Known limits and loose ends

- **Two levels of tool for interiors.** Editor brushes are boxes, wedges and cylinders with no CSG; detailed
  interiors are still better drawn in TrenchBroom, whose `.map` importer stays (design 15 §10a).
- **The Brushes panel and the Assets panel do not meet.** #61 was written before #366 merged, so a brush's
  material is chosen from a list of `material` records in the Brushes panel, not dragged from Assets.
- **Pitch, roll and scale are a placement's only.** A prefab's `children`, far proxies and travel entries still
  carry a yaw, and `ed_make_prefab` refuses a placement that is tipped or scaled for that reason.
- **Water from the terrain tools is physical only.** A `water_volume` is placed over a sector with water, so you
  can swim, but the client does not draw it.
- **The modders' editor is the Development host.** It brings the Development configuration's dev cvars and
  commands; no Shipping-with-editor configuration was made.

## 5. Traps

- **A new editor panel is two lines, or CI does not draw it.** Add it to `DevTools.ListPanels()` and give it a
  dock line in `EditorLayout.BuildDefault`; `ed_panel all` then brings it forward in the smoke runs, and a panel
  that throws fails them.
- **The log is per app since #49.** `TestEnv.Unique` is gone. A test's `CaptureSink()` made outside an app is a
  log of the test's own, current for the rest of the test, and apps made afterwards log into it; made while an
  app is current, it is that app's. `ProcessWideState` is now only for tests that change
  `AppEnvironment.Process` or another true global.
- **A non-owning app's log cvars now do something.** `developer`, `log_file_level`, `log_keep` and
  `log_queue_size` configure that app's own log when changed (they used to be ignored); only the owner applies
  their defaults at start.
- **`'` starts a raw console argument.** A script that passed a value beginning with a single quote now gets
  everything up to the next `'` as one token; `CommandLine.Quote` double-quotes such a value.
- **A reorder rewrites the object whole.** `JsonFileEdit.PatchRecord` writes an object in its new order when its
  keys were reordered (so a moved state is saved where it was moved); a patch-file save still merges objects and
  cannot reorder.
- **Merge order mattered.** #61 branched before #366 merged (§2), and #375 merged `main` twice; when two issues
  touch `DevTools`, `EditorLayout` or `Sage.Editing`'s `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt`, the second rebases on the
  first and keeps both sets of lines.
- **#375's commands are checked against the registry dump.** Until PR #547 merges, `ed_tabs`, `ed_mod` and the
  rest are not in `main`'s dump, and `check_docs.py` reports them in design 15 §10x; it passes once #547 is in.
