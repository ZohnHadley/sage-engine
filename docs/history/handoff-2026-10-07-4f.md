## 2. Known limits and loose ends

- **Theft is an event nobody punishes.** `Stolen` carries the thief, the container, the item, the owner and the
  faction; crime, witnesses and bounty (#389, 4r) are what will read it.
- **The Sandbox does not show most of 4f.** Its watcher drops loot, but it has no chest, no merchant (the
  hermit's shop is the #99 shell), no skills and no perks. The two exit games and `tests/games/kit-screens`
  (which has the perks hall) are where 4f can be seen.
- **Condition is half-shown.** Weapons deal less as they wear and armour wears and comes off, but armour's
  effects do not shrink with condition, and the kit's grid does not draw a condition bar.
- **A merchant restocks lazily.** `Merchants.Ready` runs when a trade happens or the shop opens, not on a
  timer; a merchant nobody visits is restocked on the next visit, not on the hour.
- The backlog from 10b still holds: the Brushes panel and the Assets panel do not meet, pitch, roll and scale
  are a placement's only, the client does not draw water from the terrain tools, and the modders' editor is the
  Development host (the 10b handoff's §2).

## 5. Traps

- **#382's citations wait for #555.** Design 16, sheet 16 and this pack cite `ItemDurabilityTests` and
  `ItemBroke`; until PR #555 is in `main`, `check_docs.py` reports them as missing tests and an unknown event. It
  passes once #555 has merged.
- **Anything that moves items out of a container calls `Containers.Took`,** or a theft goes unseen. The kit's
  take-all and grid transfer do; a game's own screen or script must too.
- **Bodies are the base's now.** Since #378 a creature that dies with an inventory becomes an unowned container
  in `sage.items.containers`, and the kit's `rpg.use_screens` no longer reads `Died`; it runs after the base's
  system and opens the loot screen for any container that is not locked, with no `use_screen` part needed.
- **`ItemContainer`, not `Container`.** The component's C# name avoids `Sage.UI.Container`; its id is
  `sage:container`.
- **`attribute` is the base's condition.** #379 took the id for the leveled list's "player level"
  (plugin `sage.gameplay.attributes`); the kit's perk conditions are `has_perk`, `skill` and `level`, and a new
  word must not reuse it.
- **Loot has its own random stream.** `loot_random` is seeded from the world's name, apart from `random`, so a
  drop does not move when something else draws a number; a test that wants another drop changes the world's
  name, not the order of its calls.
- **Instances are additive in saves.** `ItemStack.Instance`, `EquippedItem.Instance` and `Pickup.Instance` are
  new fields with no `Version` bump (`"Instance": null` is written for a plain stack); the golden save
  `tests/Sage.Tests/Content/Saves/instances` is rewritten by `WriteTheGoldenInstanceSaveWhenAsked` with
  `SAGE_WRITE_GOLDEN_INSTANCE_SAVE` set.
- **Take by record takes plain units first.** `Take` and `Drop` by record leave an instance alone while there are
  plain units; code that means one unit uses the index calls (`TakeAt`, `DropAt`, `MoveTo`).
- **Perks are applied once.** A perk's effects are applied on grant and saved as attributes and active effects;
  a load does not apply them again, and a test that grants after a load is granting twice on purpose.
- **The kit asks through the vocabularies.** `Sage.Kits.Rpg.csproj` has SAGE0120 in `NoWarn` since #381, for the
  perk conditions and action.
- **`SpeedScale` 0 means unscaled.** `CharacterController.SpeedScale` is transient and written every tick from
  `speedAttribute`; a game with no `speedAttribute` leaves it 0 and walks at the profile's speed.
- **Merge order mattered.** #380 branched from #383's branch (it moves stacks with `Items.MoveTo`), and #382
  merged `main` twice; when two issues touch `Items.cs`, `GameplayModules.cs` or `Sage.Gameplay`'s
  `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt`, the second rebases on the first and keeps both sets of lines.
