## 2. Known limits and loose ends

- **Demo replays are not deterministic in the real client.** A replay is exact where the save holds everything the
  simulation reads, which is every headless test and the Sandbox booted headless. In the real Sandbox client three
  things are not in the save: the camera rig's transform is written at frame rate and saved, `FaceCameraSystem` reads
  `ActiveCamera`, and `HopSystem` keys off the tick and a runtime entity id. A follow-up issue is worth filing before
  anyone builds on replays; the TDD's risk table says the same.
- **#341's in-game rebinding UI shipped with #328** (§0). The kit's screen is reachable by `ui_open rpg:controls`; the
  Sandbox has no menu button for it, which belongs with 4q's options screens (#339).
- **The terrain pokes through the hut's floor.** Pre-existing, noticed when the planks surface went in (#327); worth an
  issue.
- **`snd_stats` lost its distance column** with #336; the `snd_debug` overlay has the distance, and `AudioMonitor`
  computes it.
- **Gameplay one-shots are not replayed on a hot reload.** A reload stops a one-shot and restarts a loop; only a
  previewed sound plays again.
- **Rows that wait on the open PRs.** The docs name tests that exist only on the branches of #331, #335 and #336:
  REQ-AUD-13 (doppler, rolloff and cones) and the audio sheet's REQ-AUD-14 (tools), REQ-INP-11 (pads and rumble),
  REQ-INP-12 (last-used device), REQ-INP-15's `ADeadZoneIsRadialAndRescaledToReachOne`, REQ-PLAT-10, and the
  cvars, commands and fields those add (`snd_debug`, `snd_doppler`, `joy_deadzone`, `joy_curve`, `joy_rumble`,
  `joy_players`, `pad_list`, `pad_assign`). `tools/check_docs.py` fails on them until the three PRs are on `main`.
- The kit's default input maps are not in the registry dump (#354), and the glyph a prompt draws for the device in
  use is the screen's (#352): the engine half, `InputGlyphs.Pick`, is built.

## 5. Traps

- **The occlusion low-pass and the reverb reach into MonoGame.** `OpenAlEffects` takes the EFX entry points from
  `alGetProcAddress` and the OpenAL source id of a playing `SoundEffectInstance` from MonoGame's internal `SourceId`
  (through an `UnsafeAccessor`). A MonoGame update that renames it does not fail: the backend logs one warning and
  plays occlusion as volume only, with no reverb. If the Sandbox's rooms go dry, look for that warning first.
- **Streaming is checked by its buffers, not by ear.** The CI tests prove the samples a streamed voice is fed and the
  allocation it does not make; nothing in CI hears the device. A frame longer than about 370 ms drains the four
  buffers and underruns.
- **A rebind is a patch, so it can empty a mod's binding.** The player's file has a patch on every map that bound the
  action, which empties it there. Resetting the action (`bind_reset`) deletes the lines and the game's and the mods'
  own come back. A test that needs a rebind uses `HeadlessAppBuilder.WithUserInput(file)`, never the real user folder.
- **`effect.cues` was removed, not renamed.** Content that still says `cues` on an `effect` has to move to
  `appliedCues` (and `tickCues`, `removedCues` where it meant those).
- **Input decisions live in `Sage.Simulation`.** Binding evaluation (`ActionMapper`), the mouse policy, the pad
  assignment, the stick response, the rumble mixer and the demo format are headless so tests reach them; the client's
  `InputActions` and `InputDevices` only adapt devices. A change to what counts as a press is a change in
  `ActionMapper`, with a test in `ActionMapperTests`.
- **Sandbox audio is generated.** `games/Sandbox/tools/make_placeholder_audio.py` writes the WAVs and the four OGG
  tracks; `fire_loop`, `rain_loop` and `thunder` are not byte-for-byte reproducible, so a re-run shows them changed.
