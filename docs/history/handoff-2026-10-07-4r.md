## 2. Known limits and loose ends

- **Crime has no consequences beyond the chase.** A guard turns hostile and walks up; there is no arrest, jail or
  yield flow (content can hang a dialogue on the captain with `pay_bounty`), a bounty never decays, and a chest in an
  owned place is not itself owned. The Sandbox has no guard, no owned place and no crime demo; `CrimeTests` and the
  kit-screens game (two ranked factions) are where 4r's crime and ranks can be seen.
- **Squads flank; they do not form up.** There are no formation shapes, and the squad blackboard is read by tasks and
  measures, not by a tree's numeric `set` and `check`. Squad state and low health are `ai_measure`s read through the
  `utility` selector, not AI conditions.
- **A creature hears one thing at a time**, and a stuck arrow cannot be picked up again. Healing is chosen only
  while an enemy is in sight.
- **Hyperlinked topics are plain list entries** in the kit's dialogue screen, and `rpg:factions` has no default key
 .
- The backlog from 4f and 10b still holds (armour's effects do not scale with condition, the grid shows no condition,
  the editor's Brushes and Assets panels do not meet).

## 5. Traps

- **#393 and #394's citations wait for #565 and #566.** Design 16, sheets 16 and 17 and this pack cite
  `SupportAndFriendlyFireTests`, `KitViewTests` and `HitboxCostTests`; until those PRs are in `main`,
  `check_docs.py` reports their tests as missing. It passes once they have merged.
- **`Quests.IsFinished` changed meaning.** Since #391 it, the `quest` condition's `finished`, `HasReached` and `atLeast`
  mean finished and not failed; `Journal.Entry.Finished` still means over. Code that asked "is it over" wants the
  entry's flag.
- **Engine AI conditions end at bit 13.** `HearNoise` and `Suspicious` took bits 12 and 13, so a game's own
  `[AICondition]`s start at 14, and the factions plugin's `target_wanted` and `target_outlaw` are declared entries
  too (16 in all; `VocabularyDeclarationTests` pins the count). A game with its own condition on bit 12 or 13 has to move it.
- **A tree has no saved position.** `AIState.tree` and the blackboard are saved, the running node is not: after a load
  a creature restarts from the root. Do not put a one-shot effect in a tree and expect it to run once.
- **Executions run when a timed effect starts.** A `damage` execution on an effect pulses once on application as well
  as each period; a poison that should start quietly needs its first period to be what it wants.
- **One filter for who is hurt.** `FactionFilter` is the question for friendly fire, an ability's `affects` and heal
  and buff targets; a new rule about allies goes into `FactionFilters.Allows`, not into a delivery.
- **A guard fights the whole `ApplyHit`.** The order is friendly-fire filter, guard (parry, block, break), damage
  pipeline, reactions. A parried blow lands nothing and spends nothing; a blocked one stops `reduction` of the damage
  and raises `Blocked` with what it stopped.
- **Noise is raised from existing events.** `NoiseSystem` runs in Phase.Late after footsteps; a custom weapon that
  fires with no `WeaponFired` makes no sound unless it calls `Noises.Make`.
- **Squads are rebuilt every tick.** `sage.ai.squad` derives `Squads` from the members each tick (Phase.Commands, before
  think) and nothing of it is saved, only `sage:squad`; do not store state on a `SquadInfo`.
- **Golden quest save.** `tests/Sage.Tests/Content/Saves/quests` is written by `WriteTheGoldenQuestSaveWhenAsked` with
  `SAGE_WRITE_GOLDEN_QUEST_SAVE` set, and must keep loading (format 4).
- **The HUD allocation test counts messages.** `SandboxHudAllocationTests.TheHudIsAScreenRecordAndAFrameOfItAllocatesNothing`
  fails when the Sandbox's fights add a HUD message ("firebug hits you for 9" was the third); #393 made the test clear
  what the fights said before its own lines. A new Sandbox fight message can break it again.
- **Merge order mattered.** #393 was branched from #390's branch (it reuses `FactionFilter`) and #388 built its
  measures on #387's `ai_measure`; when two issues touch `GameplayModules.cs`, `AI.cs`, `AIThinkSystem.cs` or
  `Sage.Gameplay`'s `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt`, the second rebases on the first and keeps both sets of lines, and
  `schemas/` is regenerated after every merge of `main`.
